隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 30 日

这份说明适用于开源 DayRail 网页版和桌面版，介绍应用实际处理哪些数据，以及数据会去哪里。

本机数据

任务、项目、习惯、时间安排、背景资料和反思记录保存在你的设备上。DayRail 没有用于接收这些内容的自有账号或应用后端。设备上的设置、Google 授权凭据和你填写的 AI API 密钥也保存在本机。

你导出或生成的本地备份可能包含规划记录和个人内容。备份文件的保存位置及分享对象由你决定。

Google Drive 同步

同步是可选功能。连接时，Google 会请求你授权 DayRail 使用 drive.appdata 权限。应用会在你自己的 Google Drive 隐藏应用数据目录中读写规划数据、历史快照和设备同步信息，用于备份、恢复和设备之间的数据合并。此权限不允许 DayRail 浏览你的其他 Drive 文件。

DayRail 也会通过 Google Drive API 读取当前账号的邮箱地址，在本机检查连接的账号是否与你此前使用的账号一致。Google 授权凭据不进入设备之间的规划数据同步。

这些请求由你的设备直接发送给 Google。规划数据和授权凭据不经过维护者的服务器。Google 按其隐私政策处理其服务收到的数据。

可选 AI 功能

AI 功能需要你自行配置服务地址、模型和密钥。发起请求时，相关任务、习惯、反思、背景资料或你粘贴的内容可能包含在请求中，直接发送到你配置的服务。来自 Google Drive 的同步记录也可能成为你选择使用的 AI 功能的输入。

DayRail 不运行自己的模型训练服务。你选择的 AI 服务如何保留、使用或删除请求，取决于该服务的政策。请在发送个人内容前检查服务地址和将要发送的数据范围。

其他网络请求

访问网页版、加载字体、检查桌面更新、登录 Google 或调用 AI 服务时，对应提供方会收到正常的网络请求信息，例如 IP 地址和请求时间。DayRail 的应用代码没有独立的广告或行为分析 SDK。网页托管、Google、GitHub 及你选择的 AI 服务分别适用其自身政策。

断开连接和删除数据

你可以在应用设置中断开 Google Drive，也可以在 Google 账号的第三方连接设置中撤销授权。断开连接会停止本设备继续同步，不会自动删除已有的本机数据、Drive 快照或你导出的备份。

这些副本需要分别管理。本机数据可通过应用的数据管理功能处理；Google Drive 中的隐藏应用数据可在 Drive 的应用管理设置中处理；导出的备份由你在保存位置管理。Google 或 AI 服务保留的其他信息由相应服务的政策决定。

联系和更新

如有数据处理问题，可以通过 DayRail 的 GitHub Issues联系维护者。请勿在公开 issue 中粘贴个人记录、授权凭据或 API 密钥。此页面会随应用数据处理方式的变化更新。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy covers the open-source DayRail web and desktop apps.

Local data

Tasks, projects, habits, schedules, background information and reflections are stored on your device. DayRail has no account or application backend that receives this content. Device settings, Google authorization credentials and your AI API keys also stay on the device.

Local backups and exports can contain personal planning content. You choose where to keep these files and whom to share them with.

Google Drive sync

Sync is optional. When you connect, Google asks you to authorize the drive.appdata scope. DayRail uses your own hidden Drive application data folder for planning data, historical snapshots and device sync metadata, supporting backup, recovery and merging changes across devices. This scope does not permit browsing your other Drive files.

DayRail also reads the current account's email address through the Drive API to check locally that it matches the account you previously connected. Google authorization credentials are not included in the planning data synchronized across devices.

Your device sends these requests directly to Google. Planning content and credentials do not pass through the maintainer's servers. Google's handling of data is governed by its Privacy Policy.

Optional AI features

You configure the AI service endpoint, model and key. When you request an AI feature, its input may include relevant tasks, habits, reflections, background information or pasted text. This content goes directly to the service you configure. Planning records synchronized from Google Drive may also be included in the AI features you choose to use.

DayRail does not operate a model training service. The service you select determines how it retains, uses or deletes requests under its own policies. Review the endpoint and the data being sent before sharing personal content.

Other network requests

Web hosting, font loading, desktop update checks, Google sign-in and AI requests expose ordinary network information, such as IP addresses and request times, to the respective providers. DayRail's application code has no separate advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. The hosting provider, Google, GitHub and your chosen AI service each apply their own policies.

Disconnecting and deleting data

You can disconnect Google Drive in DayRail or revoke access through your Google account's third-party connection settings. Disconnecting stops future sync on this device; it does not automatically delete local data, existing Drive snapshots or exported backups.

Manage these copies separately: local data through the app's data management features, hidden application data through Drive's app management settings, and exported backups wherever you stored them. Other information retained by Google or your AI provider is governed by that provider's policy.

Contact and updates

Contact the maintainer through GitHub Issues. Do not post personal records, credentials or API keys in a public issue. This page will be updated when the app's data practices change.
